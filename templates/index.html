Established in 2010, Ekanayake Motor Industries has been supplying premium automotive spare parts to vehicle owners, garages and workshops across Sri Lanka and internationally for over 15 years.

We are both a manufacturer of select rubber automotive components and an authorized distributor for Toyota, Nissan, Honda, Suzuki, Mitsubishi, Mazda, Isuzu and Daihatsu.
